## Goal

Take the given content (infer it from context) and restructure it using Barbara Minto's Pyramid Principle.

## Rules

- Lead with the answer: the conclusion or recommendation comes first, not last.
- Support the answer with key lines of reasoning grouped MECE: mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive. No overlaps, no gaps.
- Back each key line with facts or evidence as needed.
- How to frame and lay it out is your call: SCQA/SCR intro or none, deductive or inductive order, headings, prose, or tables - whatever serves the audience and medium best.
- MECE grouping of the supporting arguments is the one non-negotiable.
